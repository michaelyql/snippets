Q: A venue prints trades as a Poisson process at 6 per hour. You connect at a uniformly random instant. What is your expected wait until the next print?

A: 10 minutes. By memorylessness, the wait from any fixed instant to the next arrival is Expo(λ)Expo(\lambda)Expo(λ) with λ=6\lambda=6λ=6 per hour, so the expected wait is 1λ\frac{1}{\lambda}λ1​ hours, which is 10 minutes.
